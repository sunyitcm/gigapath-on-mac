# Primary references and official model sources

- Xu et al. Prov-GigaPath, Nature 630, 181–188 (2024). [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07441-w) · [Official repository](https://github.com/prov-gigapath/prov-gigapath) · [Model card](https://huggingface.co/prov-gigapath/prov-gigapath).
- Virchow2, arXiv:2408.00738v3 (2024), Table A2, FP16 training. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.00738v3) · [Author model card](https://huggingface.co/paige-ai/Virchow2). This refers to Virchow2, not Virchow or Virchow2G.
- Ding et al. TITAN, Nature Medicine 31, 3749–3761 (2025). [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-025-03982-3) · [Official repository](https://github.com/mahmoodlab/TITAN) · [TITAN model card](https://huggingface.co/MahmoodLab/TITAN) · [CONCH v1.5 model card](https://huggingface.co/MahmoodLab/conchv1_5). Earlier CONCH is not substituted for CONCH v1.5.
- DistillPath, arXiv:2608.17872v1 (2026). [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17872v1) · [Official implementation](https://github.com/RamonKaspar/DistillPath). Prior MPS/CUDA encoder-forward efficiency and distilled models.
- PathPool, MICCAI 2026 COMPAYL. [Author camera-ready](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026-sat/COMPAYL_079.html). Prior decoupled/shared-memory pooled serving and output consistency.
- TRIDENT/Patho-Bench, arXiv:2502.06750v1 (2025). [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.06750v1) · [Official implementation](https://github.com/mahmoodlab/TRIDENT).
- Slideflow, BMC Bioinformatics 25:134 (2024). [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12859-024-05758-x).
- PyTorch: [Numerical accuracy](https://pytorch.org/docs/stable/notes/numerical_accuracy.html) · [Autocast implementation v2.13.0](https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch/blob/v2.13.0/torch/amp/autocast_mode.py). General numerical guidance is not evidence of identical execution histories.

Official license/model pages were checked on 29 October 2026; access and terms are discussed in [third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). This is a focused reference list, not a systematic review or a claim that unreported measurements were never performed.
